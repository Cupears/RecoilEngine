I'm porting Beyond All Reason to Android through my fork of [Recoil Engine](https://github.com/beyond-all-reason/RecoilEngine). My goal is to play a full match on my phone.

I've tested starting a local skirmish, panning the terrain, and moving my 3D Armada commander by touch. The commander uses its live position and facing, and a ring shows when I've selected it.
